*Filipiñana (2026), directed by Rafael Manuel. Theatrical poster via [Kino Lorber](https://kinolorberedu.com/film/filipinana).*

> insert film blurb from Liff archive as quote here. Just main blurb bit

This is a decent new entry in the documenting-gross-inequality subgenre. I very much liked the quote from the director about golf courses being a silent violence committed against a land. 

There are parts of this that are lovely. It has an episodic, Greek epic feel about - we encounter different characters going about their lives, absurd little vignettes of life amongst the servants of the country club. 

Unfortunately it’s incredibly slow and the dramatic tension only really ramps up at the very end of the film. I don’t want to spoil anything but I felt it pulled its punches rather : it had the chance to explode from its reserved, mannered start into something quite cutting but never quite does. Still a very enjoyable watch. 

Oh, but the pine trees are not happy.
